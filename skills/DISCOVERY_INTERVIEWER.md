# Discovery Interviewer / 業務発見インタビュー

## Role and lifecycle / 役割・利用段階

- **Role:** DS leads customer discovery; FDE contributes technical follow-up after the workflow is understood.
- **Stage:** Initial discovery, account reassessment, adoption diagnosis, and exploration of the next problem.
- **Why:** Find actual decisions and friction rather than collecting hypothetical feature wishes.
- **Use when:** Interview a user about real work, reconstruct an incident, or extract decision and handoff requirements.
- **Deliver:** An evidence-labeled workflow/event map and ranked questions for value discovery or domain modeling.

## Inputs / 入力

- A role and one actual working day or recent incident.
- What is already known, with source and uncertainty.
- The purpose of the interview and boundaries on the information collected.
- Preserve the user’s supplied evidence and distinguish heard, data, and assumed statements.
- Ask the few missing questions that change the result; keep other gaps visible with an answer owner.

## Method / 進め方

- **1.** Ask the person to walk through one recent day from the start; prefer what happened over what usually happens.
- **2.** For each consequential event ask what they decided and what they looked at.
- **3.** Ask what took longest, what was repeated, and what happened next.
- **4.** Follow the work between people and systems; capture handoffs and the evidence available at each decision.
- **5.** Keep the person’s vocabulary; separate their statement from your interpretation.
- **6.** Summarize the workflow and ask them to correct it before proposing a solution; move value questions to the linked explorer.

## Check before handoff / 引き継ぎ確認

- The summary distinguishes observations and assumptions.
- Decisions identify their information inputs.
- Proposed solutions do not replace the interview evidence.
- Keep recommendations separate from human decisions and verified capabilities.
- Cite the guide section used and the project evidence supporting a consequential claim.
- Draft communications rather than sending them without explicit authorization.
- Reply in the user’s language; preserve domain terms.
