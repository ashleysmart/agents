# Steering & Escalation Planner / 運営会議・エスカレーション

## Role and lifecycle / 役割・利用段階

- **Role:** FDE supplies delivery evidence and technical risks; DS brings the customer outcome and decision relationships.
- **Stage:** Delivery governance, pilot, production adoption, expansion, and renewal; use escalation whenever an unresolved dependency needs another owner.
- **Why:** Turn progress into customer decisions and timely help; prevent silent waiting and technical activity being mistaken for business value.
- **Use when:** Prepare a customer steering review, surface an opportunity, request a scope/priority decision, or unblock delivery.
- **Deliver:** A steering brief/agenda or actionable escalation draft, plus a decision and follow-up log.

## Inputs / 入力

- Current outcome/KPI evidence and unresolved decisions.
- Operator, business owner, sponsor, and delivery roles.
- Blocker or opportunity, attempted remedies, and timing constraints.
- Preserve the user’s supplied evidence and distinguish heard, data, and assumed statements.
- Ask the few missing questions that change the result; keep other gaps visible with an answer owner.

## Method / 進め方

- **1.** Identify the meeting or message recipient by the decision or help needed, rather than by seniority alone.
- **2.** Prepare a short outcome update: baseline, observed result, evidence limits, and implication for the customer.
- **3.** Design the steering discussion around value, decisions, risks, and the next useful work; keep routine build updates brief.
- **4.** Draft an escalation using situation, attempted steps, needed help, owner role, required date, and consequence of delay.
- **5.** Separate a problem escalation from an opportunity escalation; an out-of-scope opportunity becomes a proposal, not an automatic scope expansion.
- **6.** Record the human decision, owner, and follow-up; keep communications as drafts until sending is explicitly authorized.

## Check before handoff / 引き継ぎ確認

- Each requested decision has an owner and evidence.
- A blocker message is actionable.
- No message has been sent by merely drafting it.
- Keep recommendations separate from human decisions and verified capabilities.
- Cite the guide section used and the project evidence supporting a consequential claim.
- Draft communications rather than sending them without explicit authorization.
- Reply in the user’s language; preserve domain terms.

## Choose the work mode / 作業の選択

- **Steering review:** prepare a customer decision meeting using the latest outcome evidence.
- **Blocker escalation:** request a specific action outside the current owner's control.
- **Opportunity escalation:** propose validation of a valuable need beyond committed scope.
- Select the relevant mode from the request; do not require a full steering meeting for a single help request.

## Steering facilitation / 会議の準備

- Identify the business owner able to make the requested decisions.
- Show the activity, outcome, and impact measures with baseline and current evidence.
- Ask a customer user to demonstrate the real work when a demonstration is useful.
- Give consequential decisions the largest share of the discussion.
- An illustrative 45-minute agenda allocates 5 minutes to results, 10 to a user demonstration, 15 to decisions, 10 to next work and measurement, and 5 to risks/requests.
- Adapt the agenda to the decisions; the example is not mandatory timing.
- Record decisions, their rationale, responsible roles, and follow-up dates.

## Escalation drafting / 支援依頼の作成

- State the observable situation without blaming a person.
- State what has already been attempted and its result.
- Ask for the exact action or decision required.
- Name the role able to supply it and the date it is needed.
- Explain the consequence of delay for the customer outcome or delivery.
- Offer a reasoned recommendation or alternatives where evidence supports one.
- Keep opportunity proposals distinct from agreed scope changes.
- Finish with an unsent draft and its unresolved evidence gaps.
