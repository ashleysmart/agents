# Account Growth Planner / アカウント成長計画

## Role and lifecycle / 役割・利用段階

- **Role:** DS leads account value and expansion; FDE validates reusable technical foundations and new dependencies.
- **Stage:** After an initial useful deployment; revisit during adoption, expansion, and account planning.
- **Why:** Make the next customer result easier through real reuse rather than producing an unconnected upsell list.
- **Use when:** Plan now/next/later work, identify reused capabilities, or connect account learning to product opportunities.
- **Deliver:** A value-backed growth plan with reuse edges, dependencies, and a next validation step.

## Inputs / 入力

- What is running now, who uses it, and its observed outcome.
- Possible next problems and the roles who want them solved.
- Reusable data, integrations, objects, workflows, skills, and known constraints.
- Preserve the user’s supplied evidence and distinguish heard, data, and assumed statements.
- Ask the few missing questions that change the result; keep other gaps visible with an answer owner.

## Method / 進め方

- **1.** Document the current working layer and what it actually makes available; do not assume deployed means adopted.
- **2.** Identify the next customer problem and the role who wants it solved.
- **3.** Draw which existing capabilities the next layer reuses; distinguish reused assets from new dependencies.
- **4.** Connect the next layer to a customer-owned value measure and a source for its baseline.
- **5.** Sketch what that layer could enable later; label later opportunities as hypotheses rather than committed scope.
- **6.** Identify one lesson or capability that may benefit other accounts and route it to product review.

## Check before handoff / 引き継ぎ確認

- The next layer reuses something concrete.
- A customer role owns the expected value.
- Later layers remain distinguishable from agreed scope.
- Keep recommendations separate from human decisions and verified capabilities.
- Cite the guide section used and the project evidence supporting a consequential claim.
- Draft communications rather than sending them without explicit authorization.
- Reply in the user’s language; preserve domain terms.
