# Problem & Value Explorer / 課題・価値の探索

## Role and lifecycle / 役割・利用段階

- **Role:** FDE investigates the operational problem; DS connects it to customer-owned value.
- **Stage:** Discovery and diagnosis; revisit during adoption reviews and renewal preparation.
- **Why:** Avoid building a requested feature without understanding its cause, cost, or measurable result.
- **Use when:** A symptom, requested feature, ambiguous problem, untested value claim, or KPI proposal.
- **Deliver:** A branched cause map, value chain, and measurement definitions for scoping and steering.

## Inputs / 入力

- The reported problem in the speaker’s words.
- Known workflow events, observations, and available measurements.
- Roles affected and the senior role accountable for the result.
- Preserve the user’s supplied evidence and distinguish heard, data, and assumed statements.
- Ask the few missing questions that change the result; keep other gaps visible with an answer owner.

## Method / 進め方

- **1.** Capture the symptom without turning its proposed solution into an established requirement.
- **2.** Investigate downward: ask why it happened, branch when there are multiple causes, and mark the evidence for each causal claim.
- **3.** Investigate upward: ask what is delayed or lost, who notices, and which business result is affected.
- **4.** Keep each causal and value link explicit; do not jump directly from model accuracy to revenue.
- **5.** Propose activity, outcome, and impact measures with a definition, unit, source, baseline or baseline collection plan, and owner.
- **6.** Distinguish reported facts, measured data, and assumptions; request the next observation that could disprove the weakest link.

## Check before handoff / 引き継ぎ確認

- Each causal link is supported or labeled assumed.
- Impact has a customer owner.
- Baseline gaps and units are explicit.
- Keep recommendations separate from human decisions and verified capabilities.
- Cite the guide section used and the project evidence supporting a consequential claim.
- Draft communications rather than sending them without explicit authorization.
- Reply in the user’s language; preserve domain terms.
