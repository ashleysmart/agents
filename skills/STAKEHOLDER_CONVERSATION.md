# Stakeholder & Conversation Planner / 関係者・対話計画

## Role and lifecycle / 役割・利用段階

- **Role:** DS maps the customer decision network and prepares the conversation; FDE contributes the technical evidence relevant to that decision.
- **Stage:** Discovery, scope agreement, stuck pilots, production approval, expansion, and renewal.
- **Why:** Reach the people able to act while understanding their concerns without inventing motives.
- **Use when:** Map stakeholder roles/influence, test a champion, or rehearse a difficult customer conversation.
- **Deliver:** An evidence-labeled role map, conversation plan, and specific next-step request.

## Inputs / 入力

- The decision needed and who uses, supports, funds, permits, or opposes the work.
- Observed influence/access relationships and their evidence.
- The difficult message, the counterpart’s role, and the desired next step.
- Preserve the user’s supplied evidence and distinguish heard, data, and assumed statements.
- Ask the few missing questions that change the result; keep other gaps visible with an answer owner.

## Method / 進め方

- **1.** Map operator, champion, decider, gatekeeper, and blocker roles; separate observed influence from assumptions about attitude.
- **2.** Test whether a proposed champion can reach the decider, carries the case internally, and shares relevant information without prompting.
- **3.** Plan a credible introduction through someone with standing; treat that as relationship guidance, not authority to contact anyone.
- **4.** For the conversation, separate each side’s account of events from possible feelings and identity concerns; treat inner motives as hypotheses.
- **5.** Draft a loop that reflects what you heard and asks whether you understood correctly before answering.
- **6.** Use a supplied customer-relevant number or an explicit measurement gap; request one concrete next step.
- **7.** Rehearse and give feedback on looping, use of a relevant number, and specificity of the next step.

## Check before handoff / 引き継ぎ確認

- Stakeholder claims carry evidence labels.
- The conversation checks understanding before arguing.
- The next step is specific and remains a draft until authorized.
- Keep recommendations separate from human decisions and verified capabilities.
- Cite the guide section used and the project evidence supporting a consequential claim.
- Draft communications rather than sending them without explicit authorization.
- Reply in the user’s language; preserve domain terms.
