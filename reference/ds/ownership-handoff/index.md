# Ownership & Handoff Designer / 責任分担・引き継ぎ

## Purpose / 目的

- Make outcome ownership, decision rights, and delivery responsibilities explicit across team boundaries.
- **Format:** agent-assisted work tool; these guides do not provision an application or execute external systems.

## Bring / 入力

- Customer outcome, lifecycle stage, and current roles.
- A boundary dispute or handoff that needs to work.
- Known agreements and unresolved ownership questions.

## How to run / 進め方

- **1.** Name who owns the customer outcome even when other people deliver parts of the work.
- **2.** Separate who decides from who performs each activity; include business scoping, technical design/build, commercial commitments, adoption, and maintenance.
- **3.** Draft a boundary in plain language: what one role owns and what the adjacent role owns.
- **4.** Test the boundary against a concrete scenario such as an unscoped sales promise, declining usage, or an expansion request.
- **5.** Record contradictory responsibilities and the roles whose agreement would resolve them; do not invent a final organization policy.
- **6.** Create a handoff checklist that preserves the problem, intended outcome, evidence, open questions, and receiving owner.

## Check the result / 確認

- Each important decision has a named role or an explicit gap.
- The receiving team can explain what it is taking over.
- Draft boundaries are labeled proposed until agreed.
