# Ownership & Handoff Designer / 責任分担・引き継ぎ — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Responsibility map | Activity/stage; decision owner; delivery role; consulted roles; evidence of agreement. | Not supplied |
| Boundary statement | Role A responsibility; role B responsibility; shared interface. | Not supplied |
| Scenario test | Situation; proposed decider; proposed doer; contradiction; resolution needed. | Not supplied |
| Handoff | Outcome; scope; evidence; constraints; open questions; sender/receiver roles. | Not supplied |
| Agreement status | Agreed / proposed / unknown; role able to confirm. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- Each important decision has a named role or an explicit gap.
- The receiving team can explain what it is taking over.
- Draft boundaries are labeled proposed until agreed.
