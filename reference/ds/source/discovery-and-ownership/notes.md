## Written notes / 講義メモ

- A DS maintains the relationship in order to deliver and grow the customer outcome.
- Working activity includes observing work, interviewing, scoping, showing deployed capability, and helping adoption.
- Delivery on time is necessary but does not establish that the work meets the customer’s goals.
- Ask whether a more valuable problem has emerged instead of holding to a low-value initial request.
- Keep attention on deployment cost and the account’s ability to sustain delivery.
- The interview follows a concrete day, the decisions made, information consulted, repeated work, and subsequent result.
- Ask whether the customer works differently, whether they would notice the capability’s absence, and whether the value reaches the people who decide resources.
- Organizational models are references to adapt, not templates to copy.
- Team arrangements can use account teams, shared pools, or industry groups; each has continuity, cost, and coverage trade-offs.
- Separate who decides from who performs the work at handoff boundaries.
