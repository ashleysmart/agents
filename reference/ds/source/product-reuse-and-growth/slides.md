## Slide 13

- THREE CATEGORIES Bespoke, product candidate, standard
- What it is Signal What the DS does
- Bespoke Logic only this customer needs Their own rules, data or process; nobody else asks
- Build it in the field. Write down why. Look again if a second customer asks
- Product candidate A need several customers share, built more than once by hand
- Write a product candidate note. The product team decides
- The same underlying need in two or three accounts; FDE time spent each time
- Standard The product already does it The customer did not know, or it was not set up
- Configure it and show them. Tell CS. Build nothing
- Bespoke is not a failure. Some customers should have logic nobody else has.

## Slide 14

- THE THREE CLIENT RULE The Three Client Rule, and its limits
- THE RULE ( W 1 )
- LIMIT 1
- LIMIT 2
- WHO DECIDES
- The product team
- Three customers, same need
- Count customers, not requests
- Three is a signal to look
- If three customers need the same custom work, it should become part of the product.
- Three requests from one customer is one customer. Three requests in different words may be the same need.
- Not an order to build. Some needs are common but cheap to configure; some are rare but costly every time.

## Slide 25

- THE FLYWHEEL Why it builds on itself
- The word comes from [identity redacted] 's Good to Great (2001): a heavy wheel that is hard to start and easier to turn with every push. [identity redacted]  drew its strategy this way.

### Diagram transcription / 図の説明

- Growth loop: real work → captured decisions/corrections/domain knowledge → more useful and accurate capability → a cheaper starting point for the next problem → more real work. Clean pipelines and a model able to represent change support the loop. It is not automatic compounding without adoption and verified reuse.

- Layer 1: now An agent drafts change summaries for document revisions. The QA team uses it every week
- First try at layer 2 A chatbot for the sales team. It reuses nothing from layer 1, and nobody has asked for it. Fails two of the three checks
