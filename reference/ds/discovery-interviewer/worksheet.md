# Discovery Interviewer / 業務発見インタビュー — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Interview frame | Role; day/incident context; purpose; known constraints. | Not supplied |
| Event log | Sequence; event; actor role; decision; information used; handoff. | Not supplied |
| Friction | Observed delay/repetition; consequence; evidence label. | Not supplied |
| Unknowns | Question; why it matters; role able to answer. | Not supplied |
| Playback | What was heard; interpretation; correction requested; possible next investigation. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- The summary distinguishes observations and assumptions.
- Decisions identify their information inputs.
- Proposed solutions do not replace the interview evidence.
