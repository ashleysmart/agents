# Stakeholder & Conversation Planner / 関係者・対話計画 — failure patterns / 失敗パターン

- **Use:** identify a concrete observation before applying a pattern.
- **Finding:** name the consequence and the next useful check.
- **Uncertainty:** absence of evidence is not proof that a failure occurred.

## Title mistaken for influence

- **Signal:** The formal hierarchy is treated as the real decision network.
- **Response:** Record observed access and influence separately.

## Assumed blocker

- **Signal:** A role is labeled resistant without evidence.
- **Response:** Keep the interpretation tentative and ask what the concern is.

## Facts used as feelings

- **Signal:** The assistant claims to know a person’s hidden motives.
- **Response:** Use hypotheses and a checking question.

## Discussion without movement

- **Signal:** The conversation ends with no specific request.
- **Response:** Ask for one action with a role and a date.
