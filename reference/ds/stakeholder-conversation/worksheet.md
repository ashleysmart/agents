# Stakeholder & Conversation Planner / 関係者・対話計画 — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Stakeholder map | Role; decision/influence/access relationship; evidence; unknowns. | Not supplied |
| Champion check | Access to decider; internal advocacy; unsolicited information; supporting observations. | Not supplied |
| Conversation frame | What happened; possible concerns; desired outcome; uncertain interpretations. | Not supplied |
| Draft | Loop/check; evidence or number; one next-step request. | Not supplied |
| Feedback | Observed behavior; missed behavior; revised line; next evidence to gather. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- Stakeholder claims carry evidence labels.
- The conversation checks understanding before arguing.
- The next step is specific and remains a draft until authorized.
