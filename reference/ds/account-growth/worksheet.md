# Account Growth Planner / アカウント成長計画 — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Now | Working capability; users; observed outcome; reusable assets. | Not supplied |
| Next | Customer problem; requesting role; reuse edges; new dependencies; value hypothesis. | Not supplied |
| Later | Possible future capability; enabling dependency; assumption to test. | Not supplied |
| Field-to-product | Reusable need/learning; supporting evidence; candidate owner. | Not supplied |
| First move | Validation action; responsible role; expected evidence; date. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- The next layer reuses something concrete.
- A customer role owns the expected value.
- Later layers remain distinguishable from agreed scope.
