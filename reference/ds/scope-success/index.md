# Scope & Success Designer / スコープ・成功条件

## Purpose / 目的

- Turn a request or stuck PoC into a buildable first step with a customer-owned definition of success.
- **Format:** agent-assisted work tool; these guides do not provision an application or execute external systems.

## Bring / 入力

- The request in the customer’s words and the underlying problem.
- Who is affected, known consequences, timing, and the owner of the relevant metric.
- Data/access/permission/effort constraints and any draft scope or acceptance criteria.

## How to run / 進め方

- **1.** Check urgency: who is in pain, how much, what happens without change, why now, and whose number is affected; show gaps rather than inventing answers.
- **2.** Choose one useful workflow, team, and measurable result for the first step.
- **3.** Write In / Out / Not now explicitly; retain the underlying value when reducing scope.
- **4.** Check data, access, permissions, and effort with an owner role for each dependency.
- **5.** Define done through before/after, the changed decision and decision maker, the metric and measurement method, users/cadence, and ongoing owners on both sides.
- **6.** Identify the metric owner who needs to agree the result; do not imply agreement happened.
- **7.** Read the handoff as an FDE would and rank unanswered questions that prevent building or verification.

## Check the result / 確認

- The first step changes a decision or workflow.
- Done can be measured with a defined source and owner.
- The FDE can identify both the build and remaining blockers.
