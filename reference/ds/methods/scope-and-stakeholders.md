# Scope, success, and stakeholders / スコープ・成功・関係者

## Urgency / 急ぐ理由

- Identify who is affected, how much, the consequence of no change, why now, and whose business measure matters.
- A request or written requirement starts the discovery; it does not establish urgency by itself.
- Keep missing figures explicit.

## First useful step / 最初の一歩

- Select one workflow, one team, and one measurable result.
- Write In, Out, and Not now.
- Narrowing scope should preserve a useful decision or workflow change.
- Check data, access, permissions, and effort separately.
- Give each dependency an owner role and a verification action.

## Define done / 完了の定義

- Describe the before and after.
- Name the changed decision and the role making it.
- Define the business measure, baseline or collection plan, and measurement method.
- Identify users and the intended working cadence.
- Name maintenance owners on the customer and delivery sides.
- Identify who needs to agree success and record whether that agreement actually exists.
- Technical accuracy or delivery alone does not establish customer success.

## Read the handoff as an FDE / 構築する側の確認

- Ask which source is authoritative and how fresh it is.
- Ask how the output changes a real decision.
- Ask who uses it, where they work, and what correct means.
- Ask who can resolve permissions and missing evidence.
- Rank questions by their effect on building or verification.

## Stakeholders / 関係者

- **Operator:** uses the work and feels the operational problem.
- **Champion:** carries the case internally and can help reach decision makers.
- **Decider:** owns the relevant resource or business decision.
- **Gatekeeper/blocker:** controls access or raises a consequential objection.
- Separate title from observed influence.
- Treat attitudes and motives as assumptions until checked.

## Difficult conversations / 難しい対話

- Separate each side’s account of events from possible feelings and identity concerns.
- Reflect what was heard and check understanding before arguing.
- Use a supplied relevant number or an explicit measurement gap.
- Ask for one concrete next step.
- In rehearsal, check looping, the number, and the next-step request.
- Keep plans and messages as drafts until external contact is authorized.
