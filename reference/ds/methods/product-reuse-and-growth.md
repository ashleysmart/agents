# Product reuse and account growth / 製品再利用と成長

## Classifying needs / ニーズの分類

- **Standard:** the existing product already meets the need; configure and demonstrate it.
- **Bespoke:** the customer’s rules, data, or process make the work specific to that account.
- **Product candidate:** several accounts share an underlying need that has required repeated custom work.
- Similar feature wording does not establish the same business need.
- Repeated requests from one account count as one account.
- Three independent customers are a useful review signal, not an automatic order to build.
- Reuse can be a method, knowledge graph, or skill rather than software alone.

## Product-candidate evidence / 製品化候補の根拠

- State the request in the customer’s language and the underlying need.
- Identify distinct customer types and the supplied account count.
- Record what was configured or built each time.
- Show repeated effort and a transparent reuse-value calculation when inputs exist.
- State the business owner who benefits and the suggested product decision owner.
- Separate evidence collection from the roadmap decision.

## Two growth loops / 二つの循環

- Within one account, each successful layer can make the next useful layer easier.
- Across accounts, reusable learning improves the next deployment.
- A good expansion plan identifies both the next customer result and what returns to shared capability.
- A working deployment creates data, knowledge, integrations, and trust that may enable later work.

## Now, next, later / 現在・次・その後

- **Now:** record what is used and the outcome observed.
- **Next:** identify a real problem and the customer role who wants it solved.
- **Reuse:** name the existing asset or capability that makes the next layer easier.
- **Value:** connect that layer to a measure the customer owns.
- **Later:** state possible downstream opportunities as hypotheses.
- **Feedback:** identify a reusable lesson or product candidate.

## Commercial boundaries / 商業上の境界

- Do not infer licensing, ownership, price, or exclusivity from a reuse proposal.
- Those terms need the appropriate commercial decision process.
- A note about possible commercial arrangements is not an adopted policy.

## Example / 例

- Several accounts ask for approval routing with different words.
- Compare the underlying rules before counting the need as shared.
- If the same reusable pattern exists, propose a candidate with the repeated effort and open questions.
- Keep account-specific exceptions distinct from the common capability.
