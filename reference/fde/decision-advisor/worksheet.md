# Decision Advisor / 判断の整理 — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Decision | Proposed action; reason; alternatives; recommendation. | Not supplied |
| Impact | Affected workspace/systems/people; undo path; unknowns. | Not supplied |
| Consultation | Role; specific question; recommendation; evidence. | Not supplied |
| Check-in | Goal; decided; consulted; stopped; waiting; pattern. | Not supplied |
| Follow-up | Uncertainty; owner role; next evidence; date. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- Advice states its assumptions about impact and reversibility.
- Training suggestions are distinguished from actual policy.
- The check-in makes unresolved dependencies visible.
