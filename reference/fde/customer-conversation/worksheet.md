# Customer Conversation Rehearsal / 顧客対話の練習 — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Moment | Customer role; statement; preceding context; interpretation and evidence. | Not supplied |
| Questions | Question that distinguishes competing interpretations. | Not supplied |
| Response | Acknowledgement; discovery question; customer-language reframe; proof/next step. | Not supplied |
| Demonstration | Claim to show; evidence needed; demonstrator role; limitations. | Not supplied |
| Feedback | Observed move; missed move; revised response. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- The response is grounded in this customer’s work.
- The proposed proof can actually be shown.
- Rehearsal feedback identifies observable behavior.
