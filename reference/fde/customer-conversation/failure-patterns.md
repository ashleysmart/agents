# Customer Conversation Rehearsal / 顧客対話の練習 — failure patterns / 失敗パターン

- **Use:** identify a concrete observation before applying a pattern.
- **Finding:** name the consequence and the next useful check.
- **Uncertainty:** absence of evidence is not proof that a failure occurred.

## Feature fight

- **Signal:** The response criticizes a tool before understanding the work.
- **Response:** Ask what the customer is trying to accomplish.

## Mind reading

- **Signal:** Budget fatigue or resistance is assumed as fact.
- **Response:** Use a question to test the interpretation.

## Unbacked promise

- **Signal:** The response invents a price, connector, roadmap, or saving.
- **Response:** Use verified evidence and leave unknown terms open.
