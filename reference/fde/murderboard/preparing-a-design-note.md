# Preparing the board / レビュー準備

- **Use:** turn an incomplete proposal into a short document reviewers can challenge.

## Engineering note / 設計メモ

- Record the problem in the customer’s words and the affected role.
- State goals and explicit non-goals.
- Draw the proposed design and its data flow.
- Explain alternatives considered, including configuration of existing functionality.
- Describe the test set, evaluation criteria, and owner of correct answers.
- Define the conditions for handing work back to a person.
- Describe data sources, access, permissions, and security assumptions.
- Show cost assumptions per unit and under a larger workload.
- Name ongoing maintenance and adoption owners by role.
- Leave missing material as an evidence gap rather than completing it with guesses.

## Presentation brief / 提案の準備

- Identify the audience and the decision you want them to make.
- State the customer problem and the value claim.
- Identify the evidence supporting the claim.
- List the three questions you least want to be asked.
- Choose reviewer perspectives that could expose weaknesses in the decision case.

## Readiness / 準備確認

- Keep the engineering note short enough for pre-reading; the method suggests two pages.
- Share materials early enough for useful preparation; the method suggests 24 hours.
- Treat those timings as facilitation suggestions unless the project has adopted them.
- If materials are incomplete, run a preparation session and label the subsequent review’s limits.
