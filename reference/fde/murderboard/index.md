# Murderboard / マーダーボード

## Purpose / 目的

- Find the questions and failure modes that could defeat a proposal or design before the high-stakes meeting or launch.
- **Format:** agent-assisted work tool; these guides do not provision an application or execute external systems.

## Bring / 入力

- Presentation or engineering review; the real decision to be made.
- Proposal/design note, available evidence, and known constraints.
- Panel perspectives, timing, and the presenter’s hardest unanswered questions.

## How to run / 進め方

- **1.** Define the decision the board will inform and the kind of board; do not confuse presentation rehearsal with engineering acceptance.
- **2.** Prepare the short design note and assign reviewer perspectives using the linked preparation and roles guides.
- **3.** Run a premortem before discussion: assume the deployment or proposal failed and identify plausible causes.
- **4.** Ask one consequential question at a time; record the claim, answer, evidence, and any remaining uncertainty.
- **5.** Probe the weakest material assumption through a concrete scenario; distinguish missing evidence from evidence of failure.
- **6.** Summarize decision-relevant findings by consequence; leave the actual go/fix/stop decision to its accountable human owner.
- **7.** Record open items with owners, dates, and what evidence will close them; arrange a focused follow-up rather than repeating the entire board.

## Check the result / 確認

- Every material finding points to a challenged claim and evidence or an explicit gap.
- Unanswered questions stay visible.
- A human decision and an agent recommendation remain distinguishable.
