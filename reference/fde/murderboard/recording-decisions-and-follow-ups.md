# Decisions and follow-up / 判断・フォローアップ

- **Use:** make a board useful to the people who must resolve issues and to the next team.

## Decision record / 判断記録

- Record the reviewed artifact and version.
- Record the decision owner and actual decision, when supplied.
- Keep the assistant recommendation in a separate field.
- State the reasons and unresolved objections.
- Record conditions attached to the decision.

## Open item / 未解決項目

- Give the item a stable identifier.
- Link it to the challenged claim and relevant question.
- State the consequence of leaving it unresolved.
- Identify the owner role and due date when known.
- Define what evidence would close it.
- Mark unknown ownership or timing as a gap.

## Follow-up / 再確認

- Recheck the evidence against the original closure condition.
- Record whether the item is resolved, remains open, or needs a changed condition.
- Retain the reason for a changed condition.
- Link the final evidence so a later reviewer can understand the decision.
- Draft a debrief promptly; the method suggests within 24 hours.
- Keep distribution and notifications separate from writing the record.
