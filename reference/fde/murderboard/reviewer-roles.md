# Reviewer perspectives / レビュアーの視点

- **Use:** choose the perspectives that challenge this decision, rather than inviting identical reviewers.
- **Adaptation:** the following perspectives synthesize the board method into reusable agent prompts.

## Customer-facing board / 顧客向け提案

- **Business owner:** what result changes, who owns the number, and what evidence supports the value?
- **Operator:** where does this fit in the working day, and what happens in an exception?
- **Technical counterpart:** why this approach, which dependencies are real, and what must their team maintain?
- **Commercial counterpart:** what is included, what is not, and which commitments remain unagreed?

## Engineering board / 技術レビュー

- **Evaluation perspective:** which cases matter and who knows the right answers?
- **Data perspective:** are the source, meaning, freshness, and access assumptions justified?
- **Operational perspective:** how does the system fail, recover, scale, and get maintained?
- **User perspective:** when does a person intervene, and can they understand or correct the result?

## Facilitation / 進行

- Assign each participant a perspective explicitly.
- Let a small panel cover several perspectives when needed.
- Ask reviewers to challenge claims and consequences, not the presenter’s character.
- Record simulated assistant perspectives as simulated.
- Keep a decision owner distinct from the facilitator and questioners.
