# Murderboard / マーダーボード — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Board brief | Board type; decision; scope; presenter role; reviewer perspectives; materials version. | Not supplied |
| Question log | Question; challenged claim; answer; evidence locator; unresolved uncertainty. | Not supplied |
| Finding | Consequence; observation; reasoning; affected requirement; suggested next check. | Not supplied |
| Decision record | Human decision owner; decision if supplied; rationale; conditions; unresolved objections. | Not supplied |
| Follow-up | Item; owner role; due date; closure evidence; status. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- Every material finding points to a challenged claim and evidence or an explicit gap.
- Unanswered questions stay visible.
- A human decision and an agent recommendation remain distinguishable.
