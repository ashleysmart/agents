# Production readiness and positioning / 本番準備と価値の説明

## Prototype and production / 試作と本番

- A prototype can demonstrate a useful idea without demonstrating operational readiness.
- Acknowledge what the prototype already does well.
- Assess the actual intended workflow rather than demanding every possible enterprise feature.
- Check runtime permissions/audit/data handling, live source meaning and freshness, behavior/evaluations, working interfaces, and ongoing ownership.

## Gap classification / ギャップ

- **Verified existing capability:** supported by current documentation or an observed test.
- **Customer-specific work:** a change the delivery team still needs to build.
- **Agreement needed:** an operational or ownership decision that needs resolution.
- Keep unknown capability as unknown rather than assigning it to the platform category.
- Define an owner and closure evidence for each consequential gap.

## Understand the alternative / 代替の理解

- “We already have a tool” can express existing spend, poor past outcomes, ecosystem preference, satisfaction, or confidence in building internally.
- Treat those interpretations as hypotheses until a question distinguishes them.
- Ask what workflows the customer has tried and where they succeed or stall.
- Include doing nothing and building internally among the alternatives.

## Four moves / 四つの進め方

- Acknowledge what is true about the current choice.
- Ask about the work and check what the customer means.
- Reframe the remaining need in their vocabulary.
- Show relevant evidence and ask for a concrete next step.
- Keep pricing, scope, and contractual promises with the role accountable for them.

## Evidence and rehearsal / 根拠と練習

- Offer a log, test, or end-to-end demonstration that supports the specific claim.
- Do not invent a connector, roadmap commitment, measured saving, or maintenance term.
- Rehearse the response with a counterpart role.
- Evaluate observable moves rather than whether the assistant sounded persuasive.

## Example / 例

- A customer has a laptop prototype that reads a file export.
- Ask what must happen when source data changes, access differs by user, or the prototype fails.
- Show which needs are already verified and which remain implementation or ownership work.
- Avoid claiming the existing tool is inferior without evidence.
