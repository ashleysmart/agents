# Deployment Planner / 導入計画 — worksheet / ワークシート

- **Use:** fill this worksheet with the user’s evidence.
- **Unknowns:** retain “Not supplied” until information is available.
- **Status:** distinguish proposed content from an agreed decision.

| Section | What to record | Working content |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome | Which role does what differently; why that matters. | Not supplied |
| Approach | Work item; Configure/Extend/Invent; supporting evidence; alternative considered. | Not supplied |
| Dependencies | Data/system dependency; owner role; access status; next check. | Not supplied |
| Ownership | Business decision owner; technical delivery owner; adoption owner; maintenance owner. | Not supplied |
| Verification | How the changed workflow will be observed; who can validate it. | Not supplied |
| Open decisions | Unknown; effect on approach; answer owner; required date. | Not supplied |

## Before handoff / 引き継ぎ前

- A customer role can explain the intended change.
- Each custom item has a reason and an alternative.
- Delivery and post-launch ownership are explicit.
