# Deployment Planner / 導入計画

## Purpose / 目的

- Choose a delivery approach for one customer outcome before selecting technology.
- **Format:** agent-assisted work tool; these guides do not provision an application or execute external systems.

## Bring / 入力

- The customer’s requested change and the role affected.
- Current workflow, existing capabilities, constraints, and the value owner.
- Known delivery responsibilities and evidence of product fit.

## How to run / 進め方

- **1.** Restate the decision or work that should change; separate the customer’s request from the need behind it.
- **2.** Check existing product behavior against the needed workflow before proposing custom work.
- **3.** Classify each work item: Configure uses existing functionality; Extend adds customer-specific behavior; Invent creates missing capability.
- **4.** Record why each Extend or Invent item cannot be met by configuration; keep the evidence and open capability questions.
- **5.** Pair the business outcome owner with the technical delivery owner; use the linked handoff guide to identify gaps.
- **6.** Choose the smallest end-to-end deployment that can demonstrate changed work; carry maintenance and adoption responsibilities into the plan.

## Check the result / 確認

- A customer role can explain the intended change.
- Each custom item has a reason and an alternative.
- Delivery and post-launch ownership are explicit.
