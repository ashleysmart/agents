## Written notes / 講義メモ

- Existing-tool objections can concern spent budget, disappointing results, ecosystem preference, workflow gaps, or confidence in building internally.
- Ask which interpretation applies before offering an answer.
- A satisfaction measure and a business-outcome measure answer different questions.
- Positioning starts with alternatives, then distinctive capability, value with evidence, the role who cares, and the category.
- A production review asks what the prototype does well, what everyday use still needs, who owns each gap, what to say, and what can be shown.
- Leading with features can invite objections before the customer has identified a need.
- Four moves: acknowledge what is true; ask; reframe in the customer’s words; show evidence and hand over the next step.
- A rehearsal needs spoken practice and observed feedback, not only a prepared script.
