## Slide 13

- POSITIONING · ポジショニング Positioning: the idea, and where it comes from ポジショニング：考え方と、その由来

### Diagram transcription / 図の説明

- Positioning sequence: alternatives → distinctive capability → value with proof → who cares most → category. Include doing nothing and building internally as alternatives. The ladder metaphor describes how the customer categorizes a product; the aim is a relevant category, not an unsupported claim of superiority.
