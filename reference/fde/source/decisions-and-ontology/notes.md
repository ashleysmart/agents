# Decisions And Ontology — accompanying notes / 講義メモ

- **Use:** background material, separate from operational skill instructions.
- **Handling:** names and identifying details are redacted; wording is cleaned for readability.

## Written notes / 講義メモ

- Ask whether the decision can be reversed and what would be needed to restore the prior state.
- Distinguish effects on one customer, other customers, a shared product, and data security.
- A technical feasibility answer and a business-value answer are different contributions.
- Make the relevant outcome and decision owners explicit.
- Check real constraints around data handling, security, and agreed scope.
- For an ontology, ask how a change to one object could affect related objects and decisions.
- Use a hypothetical change to expose missing rules or relationships.

## Editorial clarification / 編集注

- The short notes compress consultation into a broad rule; the decision method separates reversibility, impact, and actual authority.
- An impact walkthrough is not an implemented simulation engine.
- Record uncertainty about restore behavior instead of assuming a change is reversible.
