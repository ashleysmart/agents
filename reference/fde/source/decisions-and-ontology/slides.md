### Diagram transcription / 図の説明

- Decision tree: on a stop condition, stop and report; otherwise assess whether the action can be undone. Reversible and local: decide then report; reversible and wider impact: decide then notify relevant owners. Irreversible customer commitments: consult the business owner; irreversible shared-product changes: consult the technical owner. This is a suggested decision aid, subject to actual project authority.
