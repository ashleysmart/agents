## Slide 11

- PART 1 · ROLE BOUNDARIES How the FDE differs from other roles
- Software engineer Builds one capability for every customer. The FDE builds many capabilities for one customer.
- SIer / 常駐SE Delivers the spec it was handed. The FDE questions the spec, and what it builds flows back into a product.
- Consultant Leaves a recommendation. The FDE leaves a working system in production.
- Presales / SE Proves it could work, to win the deal. The FDE makes it work, after the deal.
- DS Owns 'what is the right problem, and what is it worth'. The FDE owns 'how do we build it, and does it hold'.

## Slide 13

- Configure · 80%+

## Slide 27

- PART 2 · ORIGINS Where the murderboard comes from

- Politics, 1980s to now

- [identity redacted]  at Ames flipped the tiger team: instead of experts solving a problem together, experts try to kill a proposal together.
- The term is usually traced to the Pentagon, where officers were grilled before facing senior leadership. A navy story links it to a 'murder of crows', but that origin is not well documented.
- The aim is to strengthen the proposal. The person presenting is not the target.
