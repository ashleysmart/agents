# Steering & Escalation Planner / 運営会議・エスカレーション — failure patterns / 失敗パターン

- **Use:** identify a concrete observation before applying a pattern.
- **Finding:** name the consequence and the next useful check.
- **Uncertainty:** absence of evidence is not proof that a failure occurred.

## Status without a request

- **Signal:** The message says blocked but not what would unblock it.
- **Response:** Name the needed action and the role able to take it.

## Happy users, absent sponsor

- **Signal:** Operators approve but nobody with budget sees value.
- **Response:** Use the stakeholder plan to reach the business owner with evidence.

## Quiet scope growth

- **Signal:** An opportunity is implemented before scope is agreed.
- **Response:** Draft the opportunity and identify the scope decision owner.
