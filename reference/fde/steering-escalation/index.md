# Steering & Escalation Planner / 運営会議・エスカレーション

## Purpose / 目的

- Put evidence, risks, opportunities, and specific decisions in front of the people able to act.
- **Format:** agent-assisted work tool; these guides do not provision an application or execute external systems.

## Bring / 入力

- Current outcome/KPI evidence and unresolved decisions.
- Operator, business owner, sponsor, and delivery roles.
- Blocker or opportunity, attempted remedies, and timing constraints.

## How to run / 進め方

- **1.** Identify the meeting or message recipient by the decision or help needed, rather than by seniority alone.
- **2.** Prepare a short outcome update: baseline, observed result, evidence limits, and implication for the customer.
- **3.** Design the steering discussion around value, decisions, risks, and the next useful work; keep routine build updates brief.
- **4.** Draft an escalation using situation, attempted steps, needed help, owner role, required date, and consequence of delay.
- **5.** Separate a problem escalation from an opportunity escalation; an out-of-scope opportunity becomes a proposal, not an automatic scope expansion.
- **6.** Record the human decision, owner, and follow-up; keep communications as drafts until sending is explicitly authorized.

## Check the result / 確認

- Each requested decision has an owner and evidence.
- A blocker message is actionable.
- No message has been sent by merely drafting it.
